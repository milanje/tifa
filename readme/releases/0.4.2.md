# TIFA 0.4.2

Released 2026-11-02 (tag `v0.4.2`). [All releases](../news.md).

Version 0.4.2 gathers the work below since 0.4.1: `tifa_factor()` runs the
first ECM level before SIQS again from 74 to 83 bits, and `ecm()` builds the
plans of its levels once. The ABI is unchanged: the shared library is still
`libtifa.so.0.4`. Against 0.4.1 (time of 0.4.2 over that of 0.4.1, one
thread, `factor-bench` of 0.4.2 built against each library), on an Apple M5
Pro and on GitHub's x86-64 runner (an EPYC 9V74, Zen 4):

## 2 November 2026 &mdash; ECM: the plans of the levels built once

`ecm()` built the plan of each level it reached (the product of the prime
powers up to B1 for stage 1, the pairs of primes up to B2 for stage 2) at
each call, then freed it. The plans of the levels for factors of up to 60
bits, with their default bounds, are now built the first time a call needs
them and shared by all the calls that follow, in every thread; other bounds
still get their own. Time of the new version over that of the previous one,
one thread:

| `ecm()`, n bits / p bits | 70/20 | 80/22 | 90/25 | 100/30 | 120/35 | 128/40 | 160/40 | 200/50 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Apple M5 Pro | 0.93 | 0.94 | 0.95 | 0.96 | 0.97 | 0.98 | 0.99 | 0.99 |
| AMD EPYC 7763 (GitHub runner) | 0.87 | 0.88 | 0.90 | 0.93 | 0.94 | 0.97 | 0.98 | 0.99 |

These plans take about 300 KB, kept until the program ends. No ABI change.

## 2 November 2026 &mdash; `tifa_factor()`: the first ECM level from 74 bits

Before SIQS, `tifa_factor()` runs ECM level by level, each level whole, as
long as it fits in 20% of the time SIQS would take. From 74 to 83 bits the
first level (two curves) did not fit, so p-1 ran alone: an 80-bit number
with a 22-bit factor took 1.66 times as long as with 0.4.0, which ran these
two curves. The first level now runs whole as soon as one of its curves
fits, as 0.4.0 did from 75 bits. Balanced semiprimes of these sizes pay
for it: from 76 bits they still take 0.96 to 0.99 of their time with 0.4.0
on the M5, but 1.24 at 74 bits, where 0.4.0 did not run these curves yet.
Random composites, other sizes, `tifa_factor_with_hint()` and
`tifa_cofactor()` are unchanged.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| AMD EPYC 7763 (GitHub runner) | 0.50 | 0.49 | 0.51 | 0.83 | 0.69 | 1.00 |

| balanced semiprimes, bits | 72 | 74 | 76 | 80 | 82 | 84 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| AMD EPYC 7763 (GitHub runner) | 1.00 | 1.24 | 1.22 | 1.17 | 1.15 | 1.00 |

The slowdown given for 0.4.1 on 100-bit numbers with a 22-bit factor (1.22
over 0.4.0) was the luck of 60 numbers: on 400, 0.4.1 takes 0.97 of 0.4.0's
time, the few that reach SIQS making most of it. No ABI change.
